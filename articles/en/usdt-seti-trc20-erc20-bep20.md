# USDT: TRC20, ERC20, BEP20 and other networks

**Tether (USDT)** is a stablecoin pegged to the US dollar (1 USDT ≈ 1 USD). The same coin is issued on
**different blockchain networks**, and the network you pick affects the transfer fee, speed and
compatibility with the recipient's wallet. This is a common source of mistakes when exchanging, so let's
break it down.

## Main USDT networks

- **TRC20 (TRON)** — the most popular for peer-to-peer and exchanger transfers: low network fee and fast
  transactions. Usually chosen for cashing out and exchanging.
- **ERC20 (Ethereum)** — the "original" network with the widest compatibility, but the network fee (gas)
  is higher and depends on Ethereum load.
- **BEP20 (BNB Smart Chain)** — low fees, common on Binance-ecosystem exchanges.
- **TON, SOL (Solana), Polygon, Arbitrum, Avalanche** — newer networks with low fees; not supported
  everywhere, so confirm with the recipient.

## Why you must not mix networks up

USDT on different networks means **incompatible addresses**. Sending USDT TRC20 to a USDT ERC20 address
can lose the funds. Always match the sender's and recipient's network.

## In short

- USDT has the same value across networks, but the networks are incompatible with each other.
- TRC20 is usually cheaper for transfers; ERC20 is more universal but pricier.
- Before a transfer, confirm the network on both sides.
