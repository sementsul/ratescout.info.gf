# How to exchange Bitcoin (BTC)

**Bitcoin (BTC)** is the first and best-known cryptocurrency, with a free rate. You can exchange it for
rubles, [USDT](/en/valuta/tether-trc20/), other coins or cash via an [exchanger monitor](/en/o-servise/).

## Step 1. Pick a direction

Decide what you want to receive: rubles to a card/instant payment, cash or another crypto. Open the
[Bitcoin](/en/valuta/bitcoin/) page and choose the direction.

## Step 2. Compare exchangers

Compare the rate, **reserve** and rating. The BTC rate is free and moves — lock the amount when reserving.
How to choose an office — [7 signs of a reliable exchanger](/en/blog/kak-vybrat-obmennik/).

## Step 3. Account for the network fee

The Bitcoin network fee depends on load. For small fast payments there is
[Bitcoin LN](/en/valuta/bitcoin-ln/) (Lightning Network) with a low fee.

## Step 4. AML for a large amount

Before a large operation an [AML check](/en/blog/chto-takoe-aml-proverka/) of the address makes sense.

## Common mistakes

- Sending to an address on a different network (BTC ≠ Bitcoin LN ≠ BTCB) — verify the address format.
- Calculating with an outdated rate — BTC is volatile.
- Ignoring the exchanger's reserve.

## In short

- BTC is an asset with a free rate; the amount can change before the deal is done.
- Compare rate + reserve + rating; account for the network fee.
- For small payments consider Lightning.

Current rates — on the [Bitcoin](/en/valuta/bitcoin/) page. Compare with USDT — [Bitcoin or USDT](/en/blog/bitcoin-vs-usdt/).
