# Exchange glossary

Short definitions of the terms you meet when exchanging crypto and currencies.

## Core terms

- **Exchanger monitor** — a service that collects rates from different exchange offices and shows them in
  one place (for example, BestChange). Our site uses this data for reference.
- **Exchange direction** — the "what you send → what you receive" pair, for example USDT → Sberbank.
- **Rate** — how many units of the received currency you get per unit of the sent one.
- **Reserve** — the amount of currency an exchanger has available for a given direction.
- **Exchange office (exchanger)** — the service that performs the exchange operation itself.

## Crypto terms

- **Stablecoin** — a cryptocurrency pegged to the value of a fiat currency (USDT, USDC ≈ 1 dollar).
- **Network (blockchain)** — the environment a coin is issued and transferred on (TRC20, ERC20, BEP20,
  etc.). More in the [article on USDT networks](/en/blog/usdt-seti-trc20-erc20-bep20/).
- **Address** — a wallet identifier for receiving funds on a specific network.
- **AML check** — an assessment of a crypto address's "cleanliness". More in a [separate article](/en/blog/chto-takoe-aml-proverka/).

## Other

- **Fiat** — ordinary money (rubles, dollars, euros).
- **SBP** — the Faster Payments System in Russia.
- **Network fee** — the charge for a blockchain transfer, depends on the network and its load
  ([more](/en/blog/komissii-setey-tron-eth-bsc/)).
