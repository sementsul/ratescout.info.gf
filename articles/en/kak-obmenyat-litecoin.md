# How to exchange Litecoin (LTC)

**Litecoin (LTC)** is one of the oldest cryptocurrencies, the "silver" next to Bitcoin's "gold". Transfers
are faster and cheaper than on the Bitcoin network, so LTC is often used to move value between services. You
can exchange it for rubles, [USDT](/en/valuta/tether-trc20/), other coins or cash via the
[monitor](/en/o-servise/).

## Step 1. Pick a direction

Open the [Litecoin](/en/valuta/litecoin/) page and choose what to receive.

## Step 2. Compare exchangers

Compare the rate, **reserve** and rating. How to choose — [7 signs of a reliable exchanger](/en/blog/kak-vybrat-obmennik/).

## Step 3. Fee and network

The Litecoin network fee is usually low and transfers are fast. Verify the address — an LTC address is
incompatible with BTC.

## Step 4. AML for a large amount

Before a large operation — an [AML check](/en/blog/chto-takoe-aml-proverka/) of the address.

## Common mistakes

- Sending LTC to an address on a different network.
- Calculating with an outdated rate — LTC is volatile too.
- Ignoring the exchanger's reserve.

## In short

- LTC — fast and cheap transfers, a free rate.
- Compare rate + reserve + rating.
- Verify the network and address.

Current rates — on the [Litecoin](/en/valuta/litecoin/) page. Terms — in the [exchange glossary](/en/blog/slovar-terminov-obmena/).
