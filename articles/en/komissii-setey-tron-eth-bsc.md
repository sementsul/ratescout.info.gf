# Network fees: TRON, Ethereum, BSC

When you transfer crypto, the fee is charged by the **blockchain network** (not just the exchanger). The
fee size depends on the network and its load, and it is worth factoring in when choosing an exchange
direction.

## How fees work

- **TRON (TRC20)** — the fee is usually low and stable; the network is popular for stablecoin transfers.
- **Ethereum (ERC20)** — the fee (gas) floats and depends on network load; during high activity transfers
  are more expensive.
- **BNB Smart Chain (BEP20)** — generally low fees.

## What the final cost depends on

1. **Transfer network** — the main factor (see above).
2. **Network load** — during peak hours the ERC20 fee rises.
3. **Exchanger rate** — separate from the network fee; compared in the monitor.

## Practical takeaway

For frequent stablecoin transfers people more often pick **TRC20** or **BEP20** because of low fees. For
compatibility with Ethereum-ecosystem services they use **ERC20**, budgeting for a higher fee.
