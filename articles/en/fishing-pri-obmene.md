# Phishing and fraud in crypto exchange

Crypto exchange is a tempting target for scammers: transfers are irreversible, and the victim is often in a
hurry. Let's go over common scams and simple protection rules.

## Typical scams

- **Fake look-alike site.** A copy of a known exchanger/monitor on a similar domain. Check the address bar
  character by character.
- **Address swapping.** A virus replaces the copied [address](/en/slovar/adres/) in the clipboard with the
  scammer's. Always verify the first and last characters of the address before sending.
- **A «too good» rate.** An unrealistically good [rate](/en/slovar/kurs/) is bait. An honest exchanger has a
  reserve, rating and history.
- **Seed-phrase phishing.** A site «for recovery/verification» asks for your [seed phrase](/en/slovar/seed-fraza/).
  Never enter it anywhere for anyone — it's access to all funds.
- **P2P fraud bypassing the guarantor.** A request to transfer «directly, without escrow». Work only through
  the platform's [escrow](/en/slovar/escrow/).

## Protection rules

1. Open an exchanger from a [monitor](/en/o-servise/) with a rating, not from ads/links in private messages.
2. Check the domain and HTTPS; don't follow links from emails/chats «from support».
3. Verify the recipient's address before and after pasting.
4. Never share your seed phrase, private key or codes.
5. For incoming large crypto — an [AML check](/en/blog/chto-takoe-aml-proverka/).

## Signs of a scam

- Pressure with urgency and a «rate that's about to go».
- A request for prepayment/«unlock» before payout.
- Luring you out of the guarantor platform into private chat.
- A request for wallet access or the seed.

## In short

- Transfers are irreversible — check the address, domain and reputation in advance.
- Don't enter your seed phrase and keys anywhere for anyone.
- Work through rated exchangers and escrow.
