# Cold and hot wallets: where to store crypto

Where to keep crypto depends on the amount and how often you use it. The main split is between hot and cold
[wallets](/en/slovar/koshelek/). Let's see what to choose for the task.

## Hot wallet

Connected to the internet: a mobile or desktop app, an exchange wallet, a browser extension. Convenient for
frequent operations and exchange, but higher risk: hacking, viruses, phishing.

**For:** operational amounts you transfer or exchange often.

## Cold wallet

Stores [keys](/en/slovar/privatnyy-klyuch/) offline: a hardware wallet (device) or paper. Practically
unreachable for remote hacking, but less convenient for quick operations.

**For:** large amounts «in storage» you don't touch daily.

## A practical rule

- Small «for exchange» amounts — on a hot wallet.
- Large savings — on a [cold](/en/slovar/holodnyy-koshelek/) one.
- Never keep everything on an exchange: access can be limited, and you don't control the keys.

## What to protect first

Not the wallet itself, but the **[seed phrase](/en/slovar/seed-fraza/)** and private key. Whoever knows the
seed owns the coins. Keep it offline (on paper/metal), share it with no one, don't photograph it to the cloud.

## Common mistakes

- A screenshot of the seed phrase in the phone gallery / cloud.
- Entering the seed on suspicious sites (phishing).
- Keeping the whole amount on a hot wallet or exchange.

## In short

- Hot — convenient and risky (operational amounts); cold — safe (storage).
- Protect the seed phrase and key, not the app.
- Don't keep all funds on an exchange.
