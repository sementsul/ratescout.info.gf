# How to exchange USDT to rubles

USDT is the most popular stablecoin for cashing out to rubles: its rate is pegged to the dollar, and the
TRC20 network keeps transfer fees low. Below is how to exchange it through an
[exchanger monitor](/en/o-servise/), how to calculate the real profit and what to watch so you don't lose
on fees or run into trouble.

## In short

- For cashing out to rubles people usually pick **USDT TRC20** — the cheapest, most predictable network.
- Compare not only the rate but also the **reserve**, limits and the exchanger's rating.
- Final amount = rate − network fee − exchanger fee. Count them together.
- Match the network on both sides: sending USDT on the wrong network is a common way to lose funds.

## Step 1. Pick the USDT network

USDT exists on [several networks](/en/blog/usdt-seti-trc20-erc20-bep20/) — TRC20, ERC20, BEP20, TON and
more. For cashing out to rubles the common choice is **[USDT TRC20](/en/valuta/tether-trc20/)**: a low,
stable network fee. Make sure your coins are on the network the exchanger accepts — address and network
must match on both sides.

| USDT network | Network fee | Speed | When to use |
|--------------|-------------|-------|-------------|
| TRC20 (Tron) | low | fast | default for cashing out |
| BEP20 (BNB) | low | fast | if coins are already on BSC |
| ERC20 (Ethereum) | high | medium | only if there is no alternative |
| TON | low | fast | transfers inside the TON ecosystem |

Exact network fees change with load — check [network fees](/en/blog/komissii-setey-tron-eth-bsc/) before a
large operation.

## Step 2. Choose how to receive rubles

Decide how you want the rubles: to a bank card (e.g. [Sberbank](/en/valuta/sberbank/) or
[T-Bank](/en/valuta/tinkoff/)), via instant payments or in cash. This drives the direction and the limits.
For cards and instant payments, [bank limits](/en/blog/limity-sbp-bankov/) matter for large amounts.

## Step 3. Compare exchangers

In the monitor compare the rate, the **reserve** (enough rubles for your amount), the rating and limits.
The top rate isn't always the best — factor in reserve and the minimum. See
[how to choose an exchanger](/en/blog/kak-vybrat-obmennik/) for the full checklist.

## Step 4. Calculate the real profit

Real profit is not one rate but a formula:

**Amount received = (USDT volume − network fee) × rate − exchanger fee.**

Example of the logic (no fixed numbers, they change): exchanger A shows a slightly higher rate but charges
a large exchanger fee; exchanger B has a lower rate but no fee of its own. For a small amount B may win.
Always plug in your amount. Worked examples: [the real profit of an exchange](/en/blog/realnaya-vygoda-obmena/)
and [the spread](/en/blog/spred-pri-obmene/).

## Step 5. Do an AML check if needed

For a large amount it makes sense to run an [AML check](/en/blog/chto-takoe-aml-proverka/) of the address
to reduce the risk of "tainted" coins. For ordinary small amounts it's usually not required.

## Step 6. Exchange on the exchanger's site

Go to the chosen office, enter your ruble details and the address/network to send USDT, and follow the
exchanger's instructions. The exchange happens on their side — our site only helps compare rates. Afterwards
you can [verify the transaction by TXID](/en/blog/kak-proverit-tranzakciyu-txid/).

## Common mistakes

- **Wrong network.** Sending USDT TRC20 to an ERC20 address can lose funds. Match the network on both sides.
- **Ignoring the reserve.** Great rate, but the reserve isn't enough for your amount — the exchange stalls.
- **Forgetting the network fee.** Final amount = rate − [network fee](/en/blog/komissii-setey-tron-eth-bsc/) − exchanger fee.
- **Large amount, ignored limits.** [Bank limits](/en/blog/limity-sbp-bankov/) may not pass the whole sum at once.
- **Rushing on a phishing site.** Enter the exchanger only from the monitor; see [phishing when exchanging](/en/blog/fishing-pri-obmene/).

## FAQ

**Which USDT network for cashing out to rubles?** Usually TRC20 — low fee and high speed. BEP20 works if
your coins are already on BSC.

**Why do card and cash rates differ?** Different payout methods have different reserves and costs, so the
rate differs. Compare by your chosen payout method.

**How long does it take?** Usually minutes after network confirmation, but it depends on the exchanger,
network load and checks. Large amounts may be checked longer.

**Is it safe?** The exchange runs in offices from BestChange monitoring with ratings and reserves. For
crypto, an address AML check and care with the network and details are recommended.

- Count the final amount with the formula, not by the "top rate".
- Match the network on both sides and account for all fees.
