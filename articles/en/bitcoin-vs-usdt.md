# Bitcoin or USDT: which to choose

The two most common instruments when exchanging are **Bitcoin (BTC)** and **USDT (Tether)**. They solve
different tasks, so "which is better" depends on the goal: move money, preserve value, or take a position
in an asset with growth potential. Let's compare point by point and sum it up in a table.

## Comparison in one table

| Parameter | Bitcoin (BTC) | USDT (Tether) |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| Type | free-floating asset | [stablecoin](/en/blog/slovar-terminov-obmena/), ≈ 1 USD |
| Volatility | high | near zero |
| For moving a sum | pricier/slower, rate floats | cheap and predictable (TRC20) |
| For holding | a bet on growth | locking value "in dollars" |
| Networks | Bitcoin, [Lightning](/en/valuta/bitcoin-ln/) | [TRC20/ERC20/BEP20/TON](/en/blog/usdt-seti-trc20-erc20-bep20/) |
| Network fee | depends on load | depends on network (TRC20 cheap) |

## The key difference — volatility

- **USDT** is a stablecoin pegged to the dollar (1 USDT ≈ 1 USD). Its value barely moves — handy for
  settlements and holding "in dollars".
- **Bitcoin** floats freely and can rise or fall by tens of percent. It's an asset, not a "digital dollar".

## For transfers

To move a sum quickly without rate risk, people usually take **USDT** on the cheap
[TRC20](/en/blog/usdt-seti-trc20-erc20-bep20/) network: a low
[network fee](/en/blog/komissii-setey-tron-eth-bsc/) and a predictable received amount. Bitcoin is pricier
and slower for transfers, and the rate can change during the transfer. For small fast payments BTC has the
[Lightning Network](/en/valuta/bitcoin-ln/).

## For holding

- Want to lock value in dollars — **USDT**.
- Ready for swings for the sake of growth potential — **Bitcoin**.
- Many use both: BTC as a long-term asset, USDT as "cash" for quick operations and locking profit.

## Fees and networks

- BTC — the Bitcoin network fee depends on load; there's [Bitcoin LN](/en/valuta/bitcoin-ln/) for small fast payments.
- USDT — the fee depends on the [network](/en/blog/usdt-seti-trc20-erc20-bep20/): TRC20/BEP20 are cheaper,
  ERC20 is pricier. Always match the network on both sides.

## Which to pick for the task

- **Send money to someone without rate risk** → USDT TRC20.
- **Cash out to rubles** → usually USDT; see [how to exchange USDT to rubles](/en/blog/kak-obmenyat-usdt-na-rubli/).
- **Hold an asset betting on growth** → Bitcoin.
- **Lock profit after a BTC rally** → move part into USDT.

## FAQ

**What's cheaper to transfer — BTC or USDT?** Usually USDT on TRC20: the network fee is lower and
predictable, and the amount doesn't change with rate swings.

**What's safer to hold?** It depends on the goal: USDT preserves dollar value, Bitcoin can rise or fall.
"Safety" here is about volatility, not network reliability.
