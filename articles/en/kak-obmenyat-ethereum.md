# How to exchange Ethereum (ETH)

**Ethereum (ETH)** is the second most popular cryptocurrency and the base network for
[ERC20](/en/blog/usdt-seti-trc20-erc20-bep20/) tokens. You can exchange ETH for rubles,
[USDT](/en/valuta/tether-trc20/), other coins or cash via an [exchanger monitor](/en/o-servise/).

## Step 1. Pick a direction

Open the [Ethereum](/en/valuta/ethereum/) page and choose what you want to receive: rubles, cash or another
crypto.

## Step 2. Compare exchangers

Compare the rate, **reserve** and rating. The ETH rate is free and moves. How to choose —
[7 signs of a reliable exchanger](/en/blog/kak-vybrat-obmennik/).

## Step 3. Account for the network fee (gas)

The Ethereum network fee (**gas**) floats and depends on load: at peak hours ETH and ERC20 token transfers
are pricier. More — [network fees](/en/blog/komissii-setey-tron-eth-bsc/).

## Step 4. AML for a large amount

Before a large operation — an [AML check](/en/blog/chto-takoe-aml-proverka/) of the address.

## Common mistakes

- Confusing the token network: ETH is the Ethereum network; USDT ERC20 is a token on the same network, but
  they are different assets.
- Transferring during high gas hours.
- Ignoring the exchanger's reserve and limits.

## In short

- ETH is an asset with a free rate and a floating gas fee.
- Plan transfers with network load in mind.
- Compare rate + reserve + rating.

Current rates — on the [Ethereum](/en/valuta/ethereum/) page. On token networks — [USDT: TRC20/ERC20/BEP20](/en/blog/usdt-seti-trc20-erc20-bep20/).
