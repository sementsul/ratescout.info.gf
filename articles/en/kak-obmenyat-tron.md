# How to exchange TRON (TRX)

**TRON (TRX)** is the cryptocurrency of the network of the same name, on which the popular
[USDT TRC20](/en/valuta/tether-trc20/) is issued. The TRON network is known for a low, stable fee, so it is
often used for transfers. You can exchange TRX itself for rubles, USDT or other coins via the
[monitor](/en/o-servise/).

## Step 1. Pick a direction

Open the [TRON](/en/valuta/tron/) page and choose what to receive.

## Step 2. Compare exchangers

Compare the rate, **reserve** and rating. How to choose — [7 signs of a reliable exchanger](/en/blog/kak-vybrat-obmennik/).

## Step 3. Don't confuse TRX and USDT TRC20

**TRX** is the coin of the TRON network. **USDT TRC20** is a stablecoin token inside the same network. These
are different assets, even though they share a network. More on USDT networks — [here](/en/blog/usdt-seti-trc20-erc20-bep20/).

## Step 4. AML for a large amount

Before a large operation — an [AML check](/en/blog/chto-takoe-aml-proverka/) of the address.

## Common mistakes

- Confusing TRX and USDT TRC20 when choosing a direction.
- Ignoring the exchanger's reserve.
- Calculating with an outdated rate.

## In short

- TRX is the coin of the TRON network with cheap transfers.
- Don't confuse TRX with USDT TRC20 — they are different assets.
- Compare rate + reserve + rating.

Current rates — on the [TRON](/en/valuta/tron/) page. On network fees — [TRON, Ethereum, BSC](/en/blog/komissii-setey-tron-eth-bsc/).
