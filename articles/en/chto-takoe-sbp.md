# SBP: the Faster Payments System

**SBP** is a Bank of Russia service for instant transfers between accounts at different banks by phone
number. Transfers work around the clock and usually arrive within seconds. When exchanging crypto to rubles,
many exchangers offer payout via SBP — it's fast and convenient.

## Why SBP is convenient for exchanging

- **Speed** — rubles arrive almost instantly, no waiting for interbank clearing.
- **By phone number** — no need to dictate a long card number.
- **Between banks** — you can receive into a convenient bank regardless of where the exchanger's account is.

## Limits and nuances

- SBP transfers have bank limits (daily/monthly) — check with your bank.
- Some banks charge a fee for transfers above the free limit.
- An exchanger may have its own minimum/maximum for SBP payout — check the direction's terms.

## How to choose an exchanger with SBP payout

Open the needed direction (e.g. [USDT TRC20](/en/valuta/tether-trc20/) → rubles), make sure the exchanger
supports SBP, and compare the rate, [reserve and rating](/en/blog/kak-vybrat-obmennik/). A step-by-step
cash-out walkthrough is in [how to exchange USDT to rubles](/en/blog/kak-obmenyat-usdt-na-rubli/).

## In short

- SBP means instant transfers between banks by phone number.
- Convenient for receiving rubles when exchanging crypto; mind your bank's limits.
- Check that the chosen exchanger pays out via SBP and compare the terms.
